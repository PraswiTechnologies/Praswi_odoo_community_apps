Direct GRN to Draft Vendor Bill (Without PO)

Clean Purchase Cost on Receipts + Native Odoo Taxes & Fiscal Position on Draft Bills

Handle spot purchases and unplanned supplier deliveries without creating backdated Purchase Orders. Record Purchase Cost on direct incoming Receipts (GRN) and generate Draft Vendor Bills where Odoo Accounting applies Fiscal Positions and Purchase Taxes natively.

Key Highlights & Capabilities

  • Auto-populates unit Purchase Cost from vendor pricelists or standard cost on standalone incoming receipts.
  • Create a Draft Vendor Bill in one click, auto-create on validation, or consolidate multiple receipts from the same vendor into one bill.
  • Supports Stock Interim (Received) accounting for products configured with automated (real-time) inventory valuation.
  • Built-in billed quantity tracking (To Bill, Partially Billed, Fully Billed) prevents over-billing.
