Direct Delivery to Draft Customer Invoice (Without SO)

Clean Sale Price on Deliveries + Native Odoo Taxes & Fiscal Position on Draft Invoices

Designed for distributors, wholesalers, and counter-dispatch teams that ship goods directly without creating a Sales Order first. Capture Sale Price and Subtotals on direct outgoing deliveries while letting Odoo Accounting apply Fiscal Positions and Sales Taxes natively on the Draft Invoice.

Key Highlights & Capabilities

  • Auto-populates unit Sale Price from customer pricelist or product list price on standalone outgoing deliveries.
  • Create a Draft Customer Invoice in one click, auto-create on validation, or consolidate multiple deliveries for the same customer into one invoice.
  • Keeps tax setup off warehouse picking screens—Odoo Accounting computes Fiscal Position and Sales Taxes natively on the Draft invoice.
  • Built-in invoiced quantity tracking (To Invoice, Partially Invoiced, Fully Invoiced) prevents duplicate billing.
